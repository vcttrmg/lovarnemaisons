Privacy Policy

Last Updated: September 29, 2026

At Lovarne ("we," "our," or "us"), we respect your privacy and are committed to protecting any personal data you share with us. This Privacy Policy outlines how we collect, use, and safeguard your information when you visit our digital catalogs, view our product listings, or communicate with us regarding our fine jewelry collections.

When you interact with our digital platforms, browse our product pins, or initiate contact, we may collect:

2. How We Use Your Information

We use the information we collect strictly to:

  • Respond to your custom fine jewelry inquiries and manage direct client consultations.

3. Third-Party Platforms (Pinterest & WhatsApp)

Our business utilizes third-party platforms, such as the Pinterest API v5, to display our jewelry catalog and connect interested buyers with our brand, and WhatsApp to handle private customer inquiries and consultations.

  • These third-party services operate under their own independent privacy policies and terms of service.
  • We do not sell, trade, or otherwise transfer your personally identifiable information to outside parties.

4. Data Security

We implement appropriate technical and organizational security measures to safeguard your personal information against unauthorized access, alteration, disclosure, or destruction.

5. Your Rights and Choices

You have the right to request access to, correction of, or deletion of any personal data you have provided to us through our communication channels.

6. Contact Us

If you have any questions, concerns, or requests regarding this Privacy Policy or your personal data, please contact us directly through our official communication channels or via our website.
